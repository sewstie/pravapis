Code: MIT licence. Stress data derived from the Belarusian Grammar Database (GrammarDB) by Aleś Bułojčyk and Uładzimir Koščanka, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Not handled yet: country names and other foreign proper nouns in inflected forms (Бельгіі, Германіі, Расіі stay unchanged — only a few nominatives are listed); words whose gender changes with the spelling (клас → кляса does not update the adjective); і → й after a vowel; genitive plural ‑аў. Words the sources disagree on are left unconverted.

Rules are not yet verified against Беларускі клясычны правапіс: Збор правілаў (2005); treat output as a draft.
